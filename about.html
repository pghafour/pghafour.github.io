About Me

I am Parsa Ghafour, a PhD student in Gravity and Cosmology with a research focus on Computational Cosmology, large-scale structure formation, cosmic web analysis, galaxy evolution, and machine learning applications in astrophysics. My work combines theoretical cosmology, numerical methods, and data-driven approaches to investigate the formation and evolution of cosmic structures across different environments.
